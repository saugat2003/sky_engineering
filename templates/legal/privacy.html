Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect account details such as name, username, email address, password credentials, profile information, team assignments, meeting records, messages, and activity needed to operate the registry.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used to authenticate users, manage teams and departments, schedule meetings, send messages, display dashboards, maintain audit history, and support administrative workflows.

3. Password and Account Security

Passwords are stored using Django's password hashing system. Administrators cannot view your password. You should use a strong password and keep it private.

4. Sharing and Access

Information may be visible to authenticated users and administrators where required by application features. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Retention

Records may be retained while the application is active or as needed for coursework, operational continuity, audit history, troubleshooting, or administrative requirements.

6. Your Choices

You may update your profile information after signing in. For account removal or corrections that are not available in the interface, contact an administrator.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, data correction requests, or account concerns, contact the system administrator or project maintainer responsible for this deployment.
